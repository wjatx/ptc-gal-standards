Delegation that only narrows

Durable agents act over weeks; the sub-agents they spawn live for minutes. The contract that keeps that safe: a sub-agent holds no standing authority of its own. It runs on a broker-computed derivative of its parent's grant, and everything it does attributes up the chain to the human.

durable · ceremony-minted, standing ephemeral · derived, short-lived identity chain refusal

1 · The agent can only ask

The parent never issues a sub-grant. It requests a delegation scope; the broker intersects that request against the parent's current authority and issues the result, or refuses. The refusal is itself an audit record.

2 · Three dimensions, none may widen

A sub-grant attenuates along all three dimensions simultaneously: oversight level, tool set, and envelope. The computation may not widen any of them.

3 · The audit chain survives the sub-agent

Sub-agents come and go inside the parent grant's lifetime. What persists is the record: every BrokeredCall a sub-agent makes carries the full delegation chain, populated by the broker from its own sub-grant record, never from anything the sub-agent asserts.

A sub-agent action is attributed to its human principal, not laundered through the sub-agent boundary.
