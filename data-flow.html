PTC · GAL · data flow · matches 0.2.1-draft

Two standards, one flow: trust context moves with the data, authority moves on a ratchet

A research agent reads an untrusted tool, then publishes a signal to a trading agent. PTC governs what the signal carries and how the receiver decides (provenance, taint, the deterministic gate). GAL governs what the receiving principal is allowed to do at all (its grant, its rung, and how that rung ever changes). Adopted standards do the plumbing: identity, signing, transport.

The PTC flow PTC

A tainted signal crosses from one agent to another and stays gated the whole way. Numbers follow the envelope.

The GAL lifecycle GAL

Authority is stored state per (principal, action-class), moved on a ratchet: promotion is recorded and human-gated, demotion is automatic and deterministic, with no model anywhere on either path.

Recommendno grant exists broker default-denies; advice is only text; no staged intent ever exists
in-looplevel: "in-loop" agent stages a real call; a human releases each act; the broker executes
on-looplevel: "on-loop" broker executes within the envelope; the human supervises the audit surface
out-of-looplevel: "out-of-loop" broker executes fully within the envelope; the human sets the envelope

Up: the promotion ceremony GAL §6.4

  1. Maker proposes a level raise, binding the evidence window into the proposal (single-use, expiring).
  2. The licensing predicate runs: pure and deterministic over durable counters plus the typed evidence artifact. Maturity ceiling, evidence present and fresh, coverage asserted, budget not in breach, minimum observations, error rate under threshold. Every ambiguous case resolves to ineligible.
  3. Checker ratifies: a different credential than the maker, compared on credential identity, so no single credential can self-promote. High-blast classes are always human-ratified per instance; detection never substitutes.
  4. Signed record before grant write: a promotion ledger record (DSSE-signed) lands first; every partial failure falls toward less authority.
  5. The grant moves one rung. A promotion is not complete until the promoted grant has acted once.

Down: automatic, no ceremony GAL §6.7

  • Four deterministic triggers, each from typed durable inputs: stale_confidence · corroboration_failure · budget_breach · false_action (one authenticated owner flag suffices). A tripped trigger drops the grant to lastSafeLevel, never below scrutiny, never to out-of-loop.
  • Lapse: a certification term (certifiedUntil) expires into lastSafeLevel with reason pending-evidence. Normative, marked not yet implemented in the reference implementation.
  • Tightening: any level to in-loop, always permitted, recorded, no ceremony. One flag can demote; no flags can promote.
  • Separate identity: demotion writes run under their own identity, and the agent has no write access of any kind to the grant store. A reachable write is a promotion bypass.
  • Re-promotion needs fresh evidence, different thresholds and a dwell time; labelLatency caps how fast a class can climb back.
Ledger recordWhat it proves
promotionwho proposed, who ratified (maker ≠ checker on credential identity), over which evidence window, under which envelopeHash
demotionwhich deterministic trigger fired and which reason applies: failing (fix it) vs pending-evidence (gather data), kept distinct end to end
bootstrapthe sanctioned seed: every grant has a ledger counterpart from birth, so the audit's no-orphan invariant holds with no exemption
tighteninga voluntary drop to in-loop, accounted for without ceremony
lapsea term expired with nothing proven broken; recorded as an absence, never as a fired trigger (not yet implemented in the reference implementation)

Where they join

PTC proves the premise; GAL moves the state. A capability's rung may never exceed what the mesh can currently prove, and the ceiling is a deterministic gate term in the promotion predicate, never a reviewer's judgment.

TIER 1 · taint-bitA taint flag propagates correct gating only if you trust the sender: low-blast, simulated effects
TIER 2 · lineageFull chain, receiver derives taint a human sees the true origin: real action with human approval
TIER 3 · signed-lineageSigned chain, verification ON the receiver cannot be lied to: acting rungs become eligible
Both acting rungs (on-loop and out-of-loop) require signed-lineage (GAL §6.13; PTC §7). And the ceiling is not the only bound: signing proves premise, not response time, so a high-blast promotion is always human-ratified regardless of tier (GAL §6.4.4). In-loop carries no provenance ceiling; it is the approval-gated floor.
