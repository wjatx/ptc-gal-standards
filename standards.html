safe-agents · standards posture

Adopt the standard where one exists; build only the seam

Everything below the two proposals is an existing standard or model that safe-agents rides, cites normatively, or descends from. The survey left exactly two seams unowned: how trust travels with data, and how autonomy becomes enforceable state. PTC and GAL are the proposals that fill them; nothing else here is ours.

Proposed PTC GAL

Both at 0.2.1-draft, extracted from a reference implementation running live since early July 2026, conformance-tested with clause-level traceability.

PTC · Provenance & Trust Context
the trust seam

A signed envelope (sender class + append-only provenance chain + taint on the Biba lattice) riding orthogonally to transport, consumed by a deterministic, model-free gate at every boundary. MCP is how agents reach tools; A2A is how agents reach agents; PTC is how trust travels across both.

GAL · Grant & Autonomy Lifecycle
the authority seam

Authority as stored, signed state per (principal, action-class): moved up only by a maker≠checker ceremony licensed by a deterministic evidence predicate, moved down automatically by four deterministic triggers, enforced by the broker per call, every change a signed append-only ledger record.

The join: a capability's autonomy rung is capped by what the mesh can currently prove about its inputs. Taint bit → trusted-sender autonomy at most; unsigned lineage → approval-gated; signed, receiver-verified lineage → acting rungs become eligible. PTC supplies the proof, GAL moves the state, and the ceiling is a gate term in the promotion predicate, never reviewer judgment.

Adopted: connectivity we ride them

Transports move calls and data; PTC rides them rather than competing (an MCP _meta field, an A2A extension, or a native channel binding).

MCPadopted

Agent-to-tool connectivity. Also governed as attack surface: discovery is untrusted input, tools are callable only via two-key admission over a signed definition set, and a changed description quarantines the tool.

Conceptual ancestry GAL descends from

Sheridan–Verplanck · SAE J3016

The levels-of-autonomy vocabulary. Forty years of taxonomy produced labels on systems; GAL turns the label into stored state an enforcement point reads per call.

FDA PCCP

A regulator pre-authorizing change inside a declared envelope: the strongest precedent for pre-authorized promotion, but paperwork, with no runtime object and no automatic rollback.

ODD

Capability claims bounded by a declared operating domain: the ancestor of the envelope and the covered-distribution evidence rule.

AP2closest precedent

The nearest thing the landscape survey found to a trust-context standard, and it is payments-only. The general seam remains unowned.
